# US-025 Keypad Beep (bEEP) — demo

Open `/?source=debug`, press the wrench (Settings) → an axis → tap `2` until the panel shows `bEEP on`; press `6`/`4` to toggle `bEEP oFF`/`bEEP on`, then scroll to `End` + `ent`. With `bEEP on` every key click sounds; with `bEEP oFF` keys are silent while the zero-approach warning (US-024) still beeps.
