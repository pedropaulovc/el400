=== AC 48.5 - Positioning-mode group readback BEFORE/AFTER real click on 'Abs/Inc' ===
Source: live ARIA tree of the 'Positioning mode' fieldset/group + radio .checked DOM state.
Action: click the momentary 'Abs/Inc' button.

--- BEFORE toggle: ARIA snapshot of group 'Positioning mode' ---
- group "Positioning mode":
  - text: Positioning mode
  - radio "abs" [checked] [disabled]
  - text: abs
  - radio "inc" [disabled]
  - text: inc
  radio .checked DOM: abs=True  inc=False

--- AFTER toggle: ARIA snapshot of group 'Positioning mode' ---
- group "Positioning mode":
  - text: Positioning mode
  - radio "abs" [disabled]
  - text: abs
  - radio "inc" [checked] [disabled]
  - text: inc
  radio .checked DOM: abs=False  inc=True
  'Abs/Inc' button aria-pressed: false

Result: the [checked] state MOVED from the 'abs' radio to the 'inc' radio
(abs: checked->unchecked, inc: unchecked->checked), so assistive tech reports the
newly-active positioning mode within the group. The momentary 'Abs/Inc' button itself
stays aria-pressed=false (state lives in the LED radio group, not a pressed toggle).
